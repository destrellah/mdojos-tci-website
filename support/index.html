PATIENT & APP ASSISTANCE

How can we assist you?

Contact MD OJOS TCI INC. for questions about using the app, general appointment information, payment receipts and technical issues.

For your privacy: Please do not email passwords, full payment card details, medical records or other sensitive health information. Our general email is not intended as a secure medical portal.

Common questions

I need help using the mobile app.

Email us with a brief description of the issue and the device you are using. Do not include your password.

I have a question about a payment or receipt.

Please contact our team by email or phone. We will advise what information is needed to locate the transaction securely.

How can I ask about clinic visits?

Use the contact details on this page for general visit information. Clinic dates and service availability may vary.

Can I request urgent medical help here?

No. This page is not monitored as an emergency service. For an urgent medical problem, seek prompt assistance from an appropriate healthcare or emergency service.

Our commitment

We aim to make it straightforward to reach our team. Response times and service hours may vary; please do not rely on email for time-sensitive medical concerns.
