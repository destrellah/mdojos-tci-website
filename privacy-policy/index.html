1. About this draft

MD OJOS TCI INC. operates in Turks and Caicos Islands. This document is a working draft for its proposed website and mobile application. It is not a final statement of actual processing practices.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the app features actually implemented, information may include contact and account details, appointment requests, support messages, transaction references and technical information. Health-related information must be separately assessed if medical features are included.

3. How information may be used

Potential purposes include providing requested services, account assistance, responding to inquiries, facilitating appointments or payments, maintaining security and meeting applicable obligations. These purposes must be confirmed against the real product.

4. Payments and third-party services

Payment processing, hosting, app notifications and analytics may involve outside service providers. The final policy must accurately name or describe the providers used, their roles and any relevant links to their policies. Do not assume that payment card details are stored by MD Ojos TCI.

5. Medical information and confidentiality

If the app collects or displays medical information, the final policy must explain what is collected, who may access it, how it is secured and how it is shared, if at all.

6. Data security and retention

Security controls, retention periods and deletion practices must be described based on verified technical arrangements, not assumptions.

7. Sharing and international transfers

The final policy must explain any actual disclosure to service providers or other authorized parties and whether information is processed or stored outside Turks and Caicos Islands.

8. Your choices and requests

Users may contact the company about their personal information. The final policy must identify the rights and request procedures that apply under relevant law.

9. Children and guardians

If children may use the app, the final policy must describe any relevant age rules, parental involvement and consent practices.

10. Updates to this policy

The final version should include a verified effective date and explain how material changes will be communicated.
