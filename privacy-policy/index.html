Privacy Policy.

How we handle personal information when you register, request appointments, and use our digital services.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy reflects the application design described by our developer as of the date above. Production hosting and security arrangements are being finalized; we will update this page as those details are confirmed.

1. Who we are

MD OJOS TCI INC. ("MD OJOS", "we", "us") operates an ophthalmology practice in Providenciales, Turks and Caicos Islands. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle personal information through our website and mobile application, including account registration, appointment requests, payments, and related support.

The mobile application is an administrative service. It does not give users access to medical records, diagnoses, prescriptions, clinical histories, or test results. Separate practices and notices may apply to information collected during clinical care outside the application.

2. Information we collect

Account information: first name, last name, email address, and a password used to access the account.

Optional contact and address details: office phone, home phone, WhatsApp number, street, house number, area, and city or island.

Optional referral information: the name and telephone number of a person who referred you.

Optional images and identification: a profile photograph and patient identification document, if you choose to provide them.

Appointments: requested appointment date, time slot, and appointment type.

Payments and invoices: amounts, payment or transaction references, and invoice details. The application may display limited card details, such as brand, last four digits, expiry, and cardholder name.

Application data: notification preferences, device and push-delivery identifiers, login session tokens and limited profile information stored on the device, and records of privacy consent and the version accepted.

If you contact us by email or through a support channel, we also process the information you provide in that communication.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to create and administer accounts; respond to appointment requests; communicate about appointments and services; facilitate payments and invoices; send requested or service-related notifications; provide support; maintain security and prevent misuse; and keep appropriate business records.

Identification documents, where provided, are used for authorized administrative and identification purposes. Access to uploaded patient ID documents is restricted to authorized MD OJOS personnel.

We use information only for the purposes described in this policy or as otherwise explained when information is collected, subject to applicable law.

4. Payments through Stripe

Card payments are processed by Stripe. The MD OJOS application does not store full payment-card numbers or CVV security codes. Stripe processes payment-card information under its own privacy and security practices.

MD OJOS may retain transaction references, amounts, invoices, and limited card display information to support payments, receipts, and customer service. For information about Stripe’s practices, visit Stripe Privacy Policy.

5. Service providers and sharing

We share or make available only the information reasonably needed for our services with the following technology providers:

Stripe — payment processing and related transactions.

OneSignal — push notification delivery, which may involve user email addresses, device identifiers, notification preferences, and delivery identifiers.

StackUp / MD OJOS backend services (api-mdojos.stackup.solutions) — application infrastructure and processing of account information, uploaded images and documents, appointments, invoices, and related records.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, to protect users or our services, or with your direction or consent. The developer has indicated that the current app does not use additional advertising or analytics SDKs to collect personal information.

6. Storage and international processing

Account, booking, invoice, notification, and uploaded document information is processed through the MD OJOS / StackUp backend. Limited session, profile, and saved card display information may also be stored on your device. Stripe and OneSignal process information for their respective services.

The production server location has not yet been finalized. The anticipated hosting country is either France or the United States. Information may therefore be processed outside Turks and Caicos Islands. We will update this policy when the final production hosting location is confirmed.

7. Security

We work with our application developer to implement safeguards appropriate to the information processed, including password hashing, HTTPS/TLS encryption for information in transit, protection of stored data, and access controls for personal information and uploaded identification documents.

The developer has advised that these controls will be verified in the production environment before launch. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. If you have concerns about the security of your account or information, please contact us.

8. Retention and account deletion

We retain account information while an account remains active and as needed to provide appointment, payment, and related services. Some invoice, transaction, or other records may need to be kept for longer where required by applicable law or legitimate recordkeeping obligations.

The application is being configured to allow users to delete their accounts directly within the app. Following an eligible deletion request, associated personal information and uploaded documents will be deleted or anonymized, subject to applicable retention requirements and the completion of the production deletion workflow.

If the in-app deletion option is not available or you need assistance, contact us at mdojos.tci.inc@hotmail.com. Data held by service providers will be handled according to the relevant service arrangements and applicable requirements.

9. Notifications and choices

If enabled, the app uses OneSignal to send push notifications related to appointments and other service communications. You may control push notifications through your device settings and, where available, the app’s notification preferences.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law and any required identity verification. You may also update information within the app where that functionality is available.

10. Children and guardians

The app’s process for accounts or appointments involving minors has not yet been finalized. Parents or guardians should contact MD OJOS before submitting a child’s personal information through the application. We will update this section if specific minor-account features are introduced.

11. Changes to this policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when our services, hosting location, security arrangements, or applicable requirements change. The current version and its last-updated date will be posted on this page. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate.
